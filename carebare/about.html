Our Mission

Eco-Friendly Commitment

We use 100% organic cotton and wool fibers in all our products. Our packaging is:

  • Recyclable and biodegradable
  • Printed with soy-based inks
  • Carbon-neutral in production

Every purchase helps plant 5 trees through our partnership with various non-profit environmental organizations.

Safety First

All products meet or exceed Canadian safety standards:

  • Choke-proof fasteners
  • BPA free
  • Non-toxic dyes

Our products are extensively tested by third-parties for lead, phthalates, and BPA.

Made in Canada

At Care Bear, we're proud to call Canada home. Our dedicated team operates from our headquarters located in the heart of Humber College's North Campus in Toronto, where innovation meets craftsmanship. Every stitch, every design, and every product we create is infused with Canadian values of quality, sustainability, and community.

We partner with local artisans and use materials sourced from Canadian suppliers to maintain the highest standards while supporting our national economy. When you choose Care Bear, you're not just getting a product - you're becoming part of our Canadian story.
